Nudge uses the tools of other systems, under your rules

Connect Nudge to outside systems that speak MCP (Model Context Protocol): a sister company's Odoo, a calendar, a chat, a document store. Nudge is the client; the outside system is the server.

Three promises

Nothing is allowed by itself

The tools a server offers come in as Not Allowed. The administrator reads each one and allows it as Read or Write, for the groups and companies he names.

A write waits for your yes

A read runs for the person at once and the answer names where it comes from. A write shows the connector, the tool and exactly what is sent, on a card, and nothing is sent before the yes.

Everything is kept

Every call is logged: who, which connector and tool, what was sent (secrets masked), the size of the answer and the time it took.

Safe on your server

  • What comes back is data, never an instruction: framed, cut to size, with the connector's secrets taken out.
  • Kill switches for every connector, a company or one connector; a company can keep connectors read-only in their first month.
  • Private Mode refuses every connector that does not run on your own network.
  • The credential is none, a bearer token or OAuth client credentials.
  • Community and Enterprise, Odoo 17, 18 and 19. No new Python package.
