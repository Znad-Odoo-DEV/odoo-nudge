Your Odoo, inside Claude Desktop and ChatGPT

Nudge MCP Server lets the assistants you already use look up your records, read your documentation and prepare changes, as you: with your rights, and with your yes before anything changes.

Three promises

As the person, never as an administrator

Each person makes a key of their own. The assistant sees only what that person may see, in the companies they work in, under the record rules they obey. Take the person out of the group and the key stops at once.

Nothing changes without a yes

Writing is a separate switch, off by default, per company. A change comes back as a token and the lines that will change; you are asked in Odoo, and the change is made only when the assistant confirms with the token after you approved it there.

Everything is kept

Every call lands in the Requests and the Changes of Nudge, marked with the name of the assistant, with its duration and outcome. The log is written by Nudge only.

What the assistant can use

Look up and read

Records of any app, the fields of the models, the screens and menus, the documentation of Odoo and your company files as resources. Ask: "Which invoices of Raghad are overdue?"

Prepare changes

Create a record, change fields, press a button, each one shown before it is made. Ask: "Create a contact for Ebrahim, then show me what you will write."

The tools for developers (code, SQL, automations, views) are never offered.

Connected in three steps

  1. An administrator turns it on in Settings, Nudge, External Assistants (MCP), and adds the people who may connect an assistant to the group External Assistant.
  2. Each of them opens Nudge, External Assistants, makes a key (shown once; valid for 1 month, 3 months or 1 year) and pastes the ready-made configuration into Claude Desktop, or the address and the key into another assistant.
  3. Press Test: Nudge calls initialize with the key, as the assistant will.

Safe on your server

  • Off until you turn it on. Keys are Odoo API keys of their own scope, hashed, with an expiry date (Odoo 18 and 19; Odoo 17 keys have no expiry and are deleted in the person's preferences).
  • No cookie and no session of Odoo: nothing to forge across sites. Wrong keys are slowed down.
  • Private mode applies: a tool that would call an AI provider outside your server is refused.
  • The budgets and the limit of answers at the same time of Nudge apply.
  • Community and Enterprise. No new Python package, no extra service: plain Odoo HTTP.

Data and external services

  • Outside AI assistants, for example Claude Desktop or ChatGPT, call your Odoo through this module, as the signed-in person and with that person's rights, after OAuth consent and with the scopes chosen.
  • It is off until a system administrator turns it on.
  • Changes are prepared and then confirmed.
  • The API keys of before (scope nudge_mcp) stop working on 2027-01-26. Connect with OAuth instead.
