A bubble on your website that answers from what you published

Products, public files and pages for everyone; their own orders and invoices for customers who are logged in. Never anything else.

What a visitor is answered from

Your shop

Published products only, with the price the website shows (taxes as the shop is set), the availability and the link of the page.

Your files and pages

The files you mark public and the text of your published pages, found by words and by meaning when the local search model is there.

Their own account

"Where is my order?", "How much do I owe?": read with the customer's own rights and the rules of the portal, with the link to the portal page.

Safe by design

  • The model has no tool to call: the facts are found by rules and handed over as data, so a page or a file cannot give it orders.
  • A limit per visitor, a daily budget of tokens per company, and private mode obeyed: no provider, or none allowed, and products and files still answer.
  • Anything else is refused politely with your contact link; nothing is created by an answer.
  • "Talk to a person" opens Live Chat or the contact form; the conversation is attached to the request only if the visitor agrees.
  • What visitors asked and could not be answered reaches your managers as Business Notes, so you know which file to add.

Data and external services

  • What an anonymous visitor writes goes to the AI provider. The chat panel tells the visitor so, with a link to the privacy policy of your company when the website has one set.
  • Answers use only published products, pages and public files.
  • There are limits per visitor and a daily budget of tokens.
