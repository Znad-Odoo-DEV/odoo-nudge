Emails that look designed, built by asking

In Developer mode, Nudge makes the emails of any app in one of four styles, with your company's colour and logo, and sends them by themselves or from a button, after you read them.

Four styles, your company in each

Choose the style: Nudge writes the rest with you. Tables and inline styles, read the same in Outlook, Gmail and on a phone, 600 pixels wide at most.

Classic

A coloured band with the title, a clean table, a square button.

Modern

A large coloured header, rounded cards, a round button.

Minimal

Quiet: a thin line of colour, plain text, a link.

Formal

A letter: the logo on top, a reference line, bordered tables, serif type.

What goes in

  • A title, a greeting and the text, with the values of the record where you want them.
  • The values of the record in a table: dates, amounts and choices in the reader's language.
  • The lines of the record, such as the products of an order, as a table.
  • A box for what the reader must answer or send back, numbered.
  • A button that opens the record in Odoo, and a closing signed by the sender.
  • The logo and the name of the company under every email, unless you say otherwise.
  • Arabic text makes the email right to left.

Seen before it is made, read before it goes

On the card, for a real record

Nudge's card shows the email as it will be read, drawn for one of your records. Nothing is made before your yes. Then a test goes to your own address only, its subject marked [Test].

By itself, on an event

An order confirmed, a request approved, a contact created: an automation rule sends the email, to the fields of the record or to addresses, with the people in copy.

From a button, after reading it

The button opens a window with the email ready for the record on screen: read it, change it, attach a file. Send does what the button it goes with does (approve, confirm, validate) and sends the email with a real Cc. Closing the window does neither.

Your rules

The original button hidden behind the new one or kept beside it; fields that must be filled before Send; the work done without the email when you allow it. Shown when, and to whom, the original button is.

Safe by construction

  • The text Nudge writes is text: it can never become code in the template.
  • The values come from fields of the record, checked for the administrator; no expression of its own.
  • What the button does runs with the rights of the person who presses it, before the email leaves; an email that fails takes the work back with it.
  • Every email built is listed under Nudge ‣ Configuration ‣ Emails with Review, switched off or deleted with its button, and taken back with the rest of what Nudge built.

Installed from Apps by an administrator, with Nudge and Discuss, Community and Enterprise; never by itself. Developer mode is switched on under Settings ‣ Nudge ‣ Nudge for Developers, for administrators.
