# Nudge: Emails

In Developer mode, Nudge makes designed emails in four styles (classic, modern, minimal, formal): the company's
colour, the values of the record in a table, its lines, a box of what the reader must answer, the logo and the
name of the company under it.

- The card shows the email as it will be read, for a real record. A test goes to the administrator's own address
  before anyone gets it.
- The email goes by itself on an event, or from a button: the button opens a window with the email ready for the
  record on screen, to read and change. Send does what the button does (approve, confirm, validate) and sends it,
  with a real Cc. Fields can be required before Send, and the work can be done without the email when the
  administrator allows it.
- The Mail Agent (off until a company turns it on) reads incoming mail and proposes what to do with it; nothing
  is done before a yes.

Depends on `nudge` and `mail`. Installed from Apps by an administrator who wants it; never by itself.
Community and Enterprise. Odoo 17, 18 and 19.
