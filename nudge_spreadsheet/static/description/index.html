Reports and comparisons, as spreadsheets

Ask Nudge for a comparison or a report and choose a spreadsheet: a formatted table with totals and a chart, opened in Odoo's spreadsheet editor.

How it goes

  1. Ask: "compare this month's salaries with last month's", "stock of product X per warehouse".
  2. Nudge asks first: in the chat, or as a spreadsheet, with a chart. The question comes only for comparisons and reports.
  3. Choose the spreadsheet: the agent reads the figures as you, builds a formatted table with totals and a chart, and opens it.
  4. It is yours to change and to download as .xlsx, and it stays under Nudge ‣ Spreadsheets.

The figures are read with your rights: what you cannot open is not in the sheet.

Installs itself

Installed by itself where Nudge and the Spreadsheet module of Odoo are both there, Community and Enterprise. With the Documents app, Nudge Spreadsheets in Documents keeps the sheets in Documents, with its editor, its versions and its sharing.
