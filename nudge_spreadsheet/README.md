# Nudge: Spreadsheets

Ask Nudge for a comparison or a report and choose a spreadsheet: it builds one from what it read, with a
formatted table, totals and a chart, and opens it in the spreadsheet editor of Odoo. What the person changes is
saved a few seconds after the last change and when they leave; Download gives the `.xlsx`.

Depends on `nudge` and `spreadsheet`; installs itself where both are there. Odoo 18 and 19 (the editor of
Odoo 17 is Enterprise only, so on 17 the reports stay in the chat).
