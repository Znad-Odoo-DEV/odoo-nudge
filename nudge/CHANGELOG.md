# Changelog

Current version: nudge 19.0.1.57.31, nudge_builder 19.0.1.0.4, nudge_messaging 19.0.1.0.4, nudge_mcp 19.0.1.0.4, nudge_ai 19.0.1.0.9, nudge_studio 19.0.1.0.9, nudge_mail 19.0.1.0.13.

## Release 2 (19.0.1.57.x)

Odoo 19 only for now: the two new companions, nudge_builder and nudge_messaging, are not ported to Odoo 17 and 18 yet; the other modules follow in the next store update.

Summary: 24 features and 2 new companions. What is on after install: answer memory (nothing is served before a manager approves one), common requests understood by rules before the AI, warnings before saving (duplicates, tax-number format, a bill number already used, the required fields), the new-user coach for 14 days (a company switch turns it off) and the weekly count of recurring questions (no AI). What is off until someone turns it on: the weekly health check, the Mail agent, WhatsApp and Telegram, the MCP server, the marketplace source and its weekly refresh. No schedule calls an AI provider by itself.

- 57.31 Fixes from the live trial with Claude: a system prompt given as one text reaches every provider whole (reading a document into a draft record, the AI over a selected list, translating a module, the AI sentences of the digest and the shop builder failed on Claude, and the other providers got it scrambled); Summarise this record works inside an answer; an approver named as an app and a rank (the purchase manager, مدير المشتريات) is found on 17, 18 and 19; a chart-only answer no longer says No answer came; answer memory is on after an install again; a health report has a readable name; the Website Builder writes theme colours that Sass compiles, so a built site no longer breaks the style of the whole website (builder 1.0.4)
- 57.30 A database older than 57.22 updates again: the migrations of 57.24 and 57.25 no longer touch the tables of the recipe marketplace and of the saved reports before the update has made them
- 57.29 Security review fixes: a yes refuses what changed since its card (a deleted or unreadable record, a field someone else wrote, a company the person left, a deleted approver) and works in the company of the card, also in the background; deleting a conversation in which the Agent acted is refused in words, since its log is kept, and it can be archived; conversations older than a set number of days are deleted every day, or archived when the Agent acted (off by default); a tool that fails tells only the kind of a database error; run_sql refuses fields kept for groups the administrator is not in and hides secrets by the column they come from, whatever the alias; run_python refuses numbers and repetitions too large for one step; a provider address must be http or https, with warnings for plain http and for a public address marked as our own server; the store page names the CC BY-SA licence of the bundled Odoo documentation (ai 1.0.9, studio 1.0.9, mcp 1.0.4, messaging 1.0.4)
- 57.28 Inside the one app, My Reports, the approvals of WhatsApp, Telegram and external assistants, the help of external assistants and the Website Builder are for Nudge managers only again; everyone else has Home, and their reports stay on its tiles (builder, MCP and messaging 1.0.3)
- 57.27 One Nudge app instead of two: it opens on My Nudge Home for every employee and Home is its first menu; the menus of managers stay with managers, while My Reports, the approvals of WhatsApp, Telegram and external assistants, and the Website Builder now reach the people their groups name, whom the managers-only app used to hide them from
- 57.26 Port gaps closed in the code (builder 1.0.2, messaging 1.0.2, MCP 1.0.2): the website builder makes its slug, writes the page arch as XML, finds a section title at any heading level or adds one, and fills the FAQ and team blocks of Odoo 17 and the stand-in snippets itself; a notice to a person goes through one helper that uses the user bus of Odoo 18 and 19 or the partner bus of Odoo 17; the debugger reads a scheduled action's failures only where the version records them; the sentences of the health, compliance, upgrade and outlier checks keep the reader's language on Odoo 17; the coach counts a screen only when every menu above it is open to the person; a test class that needs Purchase, Sales, Project, Inventory, Employees or Automation skips itself when that app is missing.
- 57.25 Review fixes (developer): My Reports is one menu that lists the reports of the person (managers read all), and the update removes the per-report menus, the rule that hid them and their columns; the builder link check follows redirects itself (three hops, each checked) within a time budget; sites carry company rules, colours and fonts are validated; the SQL constraints work on every series; old counted questions, lexicon and installed-recipe rows are collected or carry company rules; the messaging log and codes are cleaned by retention; mail the provider could not read is read again by the scheduled action.
- 57.24 Review fixes (security and performance): the messaging bot methods are private, link codes are limited and single-use, voice notes are checked before ffmpeg, saved reports refuse a menu or action sent by the caller, the mail agent takes mail only from the gateway or a manager and holds an unverified sender at the floor of confidence, builder copy reaches the page as plain text, approval fields are written only by the approval buttons; the messaging webhook and the mail gateway only keep the message and a scheduled action answers each one in its own savepoint, the website build is a background run with a lock per website, long provider work (meeting summary, module translation) runs in batches, the duplicate check and report aggregates run under a clock, and the home page is kept in the settings of the person.
- 57.23 (2026-10-17) Review fixes of the store and documentation: the Mail agent cron and the marketplace weekly cron are inactive until their switch is turned on (the default marketplace source is off), the coach has a company switch, every release 2 setting has a help text, the compliance checklists say they are a checklist, not legal advice (pack text, every finding, the Compliance screen), the README and the store page cover all 24 features.
- nudge_builder 1.0.0 fixes: access rights for the site check, its findings and the templates, company record rules on sites and checks, a store page with banner, icon and template thumbnails, the unique constraints written for the registry (`models.Constraint`), no duplicate field label, and no invented company names in the contracting, law firm and workshop templates (Your Company).

- 57.1 Shared recipes: a manager exports tours, rule tasks, presets, health settings, command words, approved answers and company files as one checksummed file, and imports it with a review step (Nudge / Configuration / Recipes). New `nudge.lexicon.entry`: words a company adds to the no-AI commands.
- 57.2 Browser speech in private mode: the company switch "Allow Browser Speech in Private Mode" (off by default) lets the browser listen, dictate and read aloud when no provider on your own server listens; Arabic dialect picker; the microphone says why when it is off.
- 57.3 Translate a module with the AI (2026-10-17): an administrator picks an installed module and a language, Nudge lists the untranslated terms, asks the provider in batches that keep placeholders and glossary words, shows a grid to accept or reject each term, writes the accepted ones with Odoo's loader and offers the .po file
- 57.4 Recurring questions (2026-10-17): questions asked are counted by their meaning in any dialect, with no AI and no person kept, and every week the ones asked often that no remembered answer or tour covers are proposed to the managers: keep an answer, record a tour or ignore
- 57.5 Charts in the chat (2026-10-17): asked in words of any dialect or chosen by the AI, Nudge draws bars, a line or a pie from the figures the person may read, with Open as report and Download PNG
- 57.6 Designed reports (2026-10-17): the printed quotation, invoice, delivery slip and payslip can be designed with the company's identity in Developer mode, as an extension view of the standard template shown with its difference and undone in one step
- 57.7 My Nudge Home: a page for each person with the numbers of the apps they may use (sales, purchases, accounting, inventory, projects, people), each with its trend against the week before, a small bar chart and **Show me**; what Nudge says (approvals waiting, questions asked again and again, the last health check); tiles moved and hidden by drag, kept for the person; counted again every five minutes as the person, with no AI.
- 57.8 Approval flows from a sentence, on Community: “a purchase order above 50,000 needs the finance manager, then the CEO above 200,000” (or the same in Arabic) becomes, after one yes in Developer mode, the fields that hold the approval, a rule that stops the confirmation with a clear message, Request approval / Approve / Refuse buttons, an activity for the approvers and a note in the chatter at each step; tried on a dummy record and undone as one set. With Studio installed its approval rules are used instead.
- 57.9 First-setup assistant (2026-10-17): on a database nobody has set up (one company with the default name, no chart of accounts, few users) Nudge offers “Set up this database with me”, from the panel, from Settings > Nudge and by sentence in any dialect of Arabic or in English. One question at a time (company, country, currency, then languages, time zone, logo, apps for the kind of business, other companies, warehouses, users with their role, fiscal year and lock date, number and date formats), each answered with a card of the exact changes and a yes: the country card names the fiscal localisation module (l10n_ae, l10n_sa, l10n_eg...) and Odoo loads its chart of accounts when it is installed; “do the rest with the defaults” after the first three; a last card with the summary, the note on the outgoing mail server (never configured by Nudge) and what to do next. Users get the groups of their role and an invitation email only if asked; what was made is archived by Undo, never deleted. With a provider it reads free text through read_setup and prepare_setup_step; with none, or in private mode, rules read the answers. For administrators only. Tests on a bare database.
- 57.10 Unusual figures (2026-10-17): the health check and a daily task kind find payslips, bills, invoices, stock moves, expenses, discounts, purchase prices and attendance far from the median of their group, with robust statistics and no AI, shown with the comparison in words and a Show me.
- 57.11 GCC compliance checklist (2026-10-17): **Nudge > Health Check > Compliance** lists the checklists for the country of the company, each with its own **Run**: UAE (VAT 5% taxes and on products, TRN of 15 digits on the company and customers, tax invoice layout, AED, WPS readiness with IBANs, employee IDs and the employer code and salaries account of the payroll settings, the Corporate Tax number), Saudi Arabia (VAT 15%, ZATCA e-invoicing module, QR code, UUID, Arabic, Hijri date note, GOSI), Bahrain (10%), Oman (5%), Qatar and Kuwait (no VAT yet), Egypt (14%, e-invoicing). Each finding has a severity, the explanation with the official reference in English and Arabic, and **Show me**; what cannot be looked at because a module is missing is said so. No AI, nothing is changed; results are kept with the health reports (field `pack`). A pack is a JSON file in `data/compliance` of Nudge or `data/nudge_compliance` of any installed module; a pack that names an unknown kind of check, a wrong parameter or severity is refused at load with a clear error.
- 57.12 Warnings before saving (2026-10-17): a small strip under the title of a form warns, without AI and without blocking, about duplicate contacts and products, tax number and phone formats, email syntax, price under cost, quantity far above the usual, past deadlines, a bill number already used and the fields the company requires; switched per company, muted per person, with Ask Nudge about this.
- nudge_builder 1.0.0 (2026-10-17): the Website Builder interviews the person, draws three directions of the home page and builds unpublished website pages from Odoo's own snippets, with 12 sector templates in Arabic and English, a shop part, a Site Check, and Tajawal (Arabic) or Montserrat (Latin) as default fonts.
- 57.13 New-user coach, no AI (2026-10-17): for a user whose first login is within 14 days, or whom a manager puts in training, the panel shows **Your first week**: how many of the key screens of their role they opened (the screens come from their groups through the menus they can see, grouped by app), the next suggested tour ("You have not opened Quotations yet: take the 2-minute tour"), the tours the manager requires, and **Done with training**. Only the action ids opened are kept, a set per user with no time per visit. Nudge / Training lists the users in training with progress, tours taken and last activity day, with Assign tours and Mark trained; Nudge / Configuration / Key Screens is the small table (app, the menus that matter) that managers extend and share in a recipe.
- 57.14 Who changed this and undo it, no AI (2026-10-17): on a record the person may change, the panel button, the command /changes or a sentence in any dialect ("مين غيّر سعر هالمنتج؟", "رجّع عنوان راغد القديم", "who changed the deadline of this task last week") lists the tracked changes newest first - date, who, field, old to new - read as the person with only the fields they may read. **Restore the old value** puts one field back and **Restore this record as it was at** all the tracked fields of one change; the card shows exactly what is written, and on yes it is a normal write with the rights, record rules and constraints of the person, logged and undone by Nudge's own undo. A state or a stage, a posted entry, the lines of a confirmed order and a done stock move are never put back, and the answer says why; a field Odoo does not track is listed as not restorable. New tools record_changes and prepare_revert.
- 57.15 Check before an upgrade (2026-10-17): an administrator picks the series (17 to 18, 18 to 19, 19 to 20) in Nudge / Health Check / Before an Upgrade and gets a report, read-only and with no AI, of what will break or needs a look: Studio objects and the logic they hide (computed fields, related paths, automations, server actions), fields renamed or removed (a bundled list per series, matched on the model of the record), `attrs`/`states`, `<tree>` and `/web#` in custom records, removed methods in scheduled and server actions, custom modules with their version and whether a migration folder exists for the target, and data the upgrade platform rejects (dangling models, orphan external ids, screens that no longer open). Each finding has its severity, count, fix and Show me; the report is kept with the health reports and says that Odoo.sh upgrades use the daily backup, so the real test belongs on a staging copy.
- 57.16 Mail agent (nudge_mail 19.0.1.0.12, 2026-10-17): Emails sorts the mail of a shared mailbox into proposals that a manager approves or rejects, and a rejection teaches a rule that is used afterwards without AI.
- nudge_messaging 19.0.1.0.0 (2026-10-17): a new companion, depends on nudge_mcp. Linked phones ask questions on WhatsApp and Telegram and get answers read-only; anything to be created or changed is only prepared, and the person approves it in Odoo through a link.
- 57.17 Report in any shape (2026-10-17): a chart, a pivot or a list asked for in words of any dialect (“طلّعلي مبيعات الشهر حسب المندوب كرسم واحفظو”, “save this as a pivot of invoices by month and customer”) is read as the person, shown on a card in words, and on yes kept as a window action under Nudge / My Reports (visible to its owner only, by a record rule on the menu), with a period that moves with the day (this month, last year, last 30 days), as many groups and measures as the view takes, an optional tile on the home page and an optional spreadsheet with its chart. Odoo checks the person's rights each time the action opens. My Reports lists them for rename and archive; a report is archived, never deleted.
- 57.18 Debugger (Developer mode, 2026-10-17): asked why an automation, a server action or a scheduled action failed, Nudge reads the definition, the logged errors, the missing fields and models, the rights and a discarded trial run, and proposes the fix as a card showing the difference on the code; the original is kept and put back on request.
- 57.19 Meeting audio summary: attach a recording (m4a, mp3, ogg, wav, webm, up to 10 MB; longer is told to be split) or record it in the panel, and ask for the summary in any dialect. Nudge listens through the provider when it accepts audio (in private mode only one on your own server), or uses the words the browser heard while recording when that is allowed, then writes the summary, decisions, action items (who, what, when as heard), dates and open questions in your language, saying which language the meeting was in. Tasks (in the project you choose), activities, calendar events, an internal note, the transcript as a company file (managers) and the recording as an attachment are each offered as drafts with their own yes. The transcript is kept nowhere and the recording is removed within the hour unless you keep it.
- 57.20 Module scaffold (Developer mode, 2026-10-17): a complete Odoo module for 17, 18 or 19 is drafted from a described need, shown as a spec card first, built and statically checked in memory after the yes, and downloaded as a ZIP that is never written to the server or installed.
- 57.21 Mobile-first panel: on a window of 600 px or less, with ?mobile=1 or in the Odoo mobile app, the panel is a full-screen sheet with a big hold-to-talk button under the message box, answers in large type, cards with full-width Yes and No, the mode switch as a segmented control, big tiles for people in the field (Clock in/out, Photo of a bill, My tasks today, Ask about this record, Record a note; chosen per company in Settings, Nudge, Mobile quick actions; a tile of an app that is not installed is not shown), the avatar hidden, swipe down to close, safe-area insets, right-to-left mirrored and dark mode. It uses the same services as the desktop panel.
- 57.22 Recipe marketplace (2026-10-17): Nudge / Configuration / Recipes / Marketplace lists the recipes of public indexes (`index.json`, format in `doc/recipes-index.md`), with the name and description in the language of the person, the kind, the apps required (missing ones marked), the Odoo versions, and Install, which downloads the file over HTTPS (10 s, 2 MB), checks its sha256 against the index and opens the import window; installed recipes are recorded and Update available shows when the index has a later date. Sources are added by managers (one default, the author's site; trusted or not, refreshed on demand or weekly). Private mode refuses the fetch unless the company switch Allow Fetching Recipe Indexes is on. Publish makes a ZIP with an index.json and the recipe files to host anywhere; nothing is uploaded. A line in Business Notes (waiting for approval) tells of a recipe that matches an app installed this month.

## 19.0.1.56.41 to 19.0.1.56.57 (2026-10-17)

Works without an AI provider

- 56.41 Answer memory: a thumbs-up kept for a manager to approve, then the same question of others answered at once with zero tokens, with **Ask the AI instead**.
- 56.42 Recorded tours: a manager records a task with **Record a tour**, anyone replays it with `/tour`; eight ready tours.
- 56.43 `/why`, `/field` and `/here` (and `/لماذا`, `/حقل`, `/هنا`) explain the screen from Odoo's metadata, in place of the error when there is no provider.
- 56.44 Rule tasks: a scheduled task done by **Rule (no AI)**: a filter, a count, sum or average, and a condition to report.
- 56.45 Private mode: a company setting and the provider flag **Runs on Our Own Server**; the server refuses every other provider before data leaves.
- 56.46 Health check: `/health`, **Nudge ‣ Health Check** and a weekly run; eleven read-only checks, each finding with a severity, a count and a Show me button.
- 56.47 Import matcher: `/import` matches the columns of a CSV or Excel file to the fields of a model, tries the file with the dry run and gives a corrected CSV.
- 56.48 Common requests without AI: Agent mode understands the usual requests by rules in Arabic, any dialect, Arabizi and English; usage rows **Understood Without AI**.
- 56.49 Local search model: an optional 135 MB multilingual model on the server finds the documentation, the files and remembered answers by meaning.

Needs an AI provider

- 56.50 Nudge: MCP Server: Claude Desktop, ChatGPT and other MCP assistants look up records and prepare changes that are made only on confirmation, off until switched on.
- 56.51 Document to draft record: a PDF, picture, Excel or CSV file or an email becomes a draft bill, invoice, quotation, purchase order, lead or contact; Undo archives or cancels, never deletes.
- 56.52 Record history summary: `Summarise this record` and `@Nudge summarize` give a dated timeline, open points, money, last contact and next step.
- 56.52 Reply draft: **Draft a reply** (Formal, Friendly, Short) or `@Nudge draft a reply` opens the email window; Nudge never sends it.
- 56.53 AI over a selected list: **Do this for the N selected** classifies, summarises, translates or fills a field, with a sample of five, the cost, one Stop and one undo.
- 56.54 Morning digest: **Set up my digest** makes a daily task whose numbers Odoo counts as the person; the AI only words them, and without it the numbers arrive alone.
- 56.54 Nudge: Website Assistant: a bubble answers visitors from published products, public files and pages, and customers about their own orders, with a limit per visitor and a daily budget.

Fixed

- 56.53 A plan on more than 20 records in the background saves its progress batch by batch, goes on after a worker died and ends where the person stopped it.
- 56.55 A field added after the name or another field of the title block shows in a group below the title, with its label.
- 56.56 In Talk mode with the screen shared, Nudge reads the total of an order or invoice and its first 50 lines.
- 56.57 A list asked for in a background task is answered in the message as a table with an **Open the list** link, not on a screen that is not there.
