## 19.0.1.59.1 (unreleased)

- Review: the Latency and Cost panel counts the days of the period in the manager's time zone, not in UTC: in Dubai the evening answers of the 24th belonged to the 25th, and the panel of "today" missed them (two tests failed every evening after 20:00 UTC).
- Review: smaller findings of the functional review. A sale line is compared with the cost of the product in its own unit of measure (a dozen at 48 against a cost of 5 a unit is under cost). A product that is not storable is no longer reported as "0 on hand" by find_products. A daily or weekly task keeps its hour across a change of summer time (the day after the clocks changed it ran an hour off), and a task every N hours counts whole hours. The card of the formats step says that the change is a setting of the language, for everyone who uses it, with no Undo. "People off today" reads "في إجازة اليوم" in Arabic. Tests: `/nudge:TestValidator`, `/nudge:TestTasks`, `/nudge:TestProducts`.
- Review: a document read into a draft is checked more before it is made. A vendor bill, purchase order or quotation of the same contact and reference as one that exists is not made twice unless the person insists (`insist`); a quotation in a currency takes a pricelist of that currency, or says there is none and no longer shows a currency it does not use; "exempt" and "zero rated" are different taxes where the company has both, a 5% line prefers the tax that is not price-included or a reverse charge and the card says when several fit; a contact whose VAT is stored with spaces or dots is found. Letters a PDF spreads apart ("INV -TRIAL-0042", "W ood V arnish") come back together on bills, invoices and quotations as they already did for price lists. Tests: `/nudge:TestDocumentToRecord`.
- Review: new products from a price list or a material list. A unit price read by the model from anything but a price list the company gives its customers is the COST (`standard_price`), whatever target the model chose (it was the sales price in two runs of three); the model says which kind of document it is. A list in a foreign currency keeps its currency on the vendor line, converts the cost to the company's currency at today's rate and says so on the card, which also says that a product with no sales price gets Odoo's default of 1.00. An "Amount" column is a quantity only in a bill of materials. Tests: `/nudge:TestDocumentCatalogue`.
- Review: a deadline of the compliance packs that a pack update moves (the UAE ASP date moved from 31 Jul to 30 Oct 2026) is told again for its new date: the notices already sent are matched by their deadline too, so a notice told at 7 days for the old date no longer silences every notice for the new one. - Review: the Setup Wizard no longer opens Nudge to every employee and sends business data out without a word. Choosing a provider outside the server turns "Nudge for All Internal Users" off, the last step says what is sent (the question and the records it needs, from everyone who uses Nudge, and that Private Mode stops it), offers masking of personal data (on by default, written to the company) and asks to tick that it is understood before Enable. Rules only and Ollama are unchanged. The wizard has two new transient fields (`redact_pii`, `data_ack`). Tests: `/nudge:TestSetupWizard`.
- Review: the morning digest and the explanation of a report no longer mix money. "Vendor bills to pay" is a positive amount (Odoo keeps the residual of a vendor bill negative); sums of orders and bills in another currency are converted to the currency of the company (at today's rate) before they are added, and Explain this report says "several currencies" instead of a total when a report mixes them (one foreign currency is written in its own). "People off today", the tasks due today, the receipts due and the open attendances use the whole local day of the person, converted to UTC, and not the instant the digest runs (a leave that starts at 09:00 was missed by a digest at 08:00). Tests: `/nudge:TestDigest`, `/nudge:TestExplainReport`.
- Review: an approval flow no longer lets a document through after it was changed: the approval holds for the amount it was given for (a new field, Approved amount, on the document), and a document edited to a larger amount has to be approved again with Request approval. The limits are now compared in the currency of the company (a purchase or sales order is converted at its date, an invoice or bill uses its total in company currency) and the messages and activities name that currency. The block no longer stops what the company did not ask to stop: scheduled actions run as OdooBot, and a website visitor or portal customer paying or signing online; the people of the back office are still stopped. Who made a document cannot approve it unless they are the only member of the approving group. The activity of the approval goes to every member of the group (up to 50), in order of id. The card says all of it. Tests: `/nudge:TestApprovalFlow`.
- Review: "Vendor invoices above 10,000 need the CFO" (also supplier invoice, purchase invoice) builds the approval on vendor bills; the word "invoice" alone still means a customer invoice, so the rule no longer blocks the posting of sales invoices by mistake. Tests: `/nudge:TestApprovalSentence`.
