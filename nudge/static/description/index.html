An AI companion that lives in the Odoo web client

It sees the screen you are on, shows you where to click, knows how your database is built, and does the work with your rights, one confirmation at a time.

What it does

Shows you, on the screen

Ask "how do I confirm this order?" or "how do I open the attendance report?" and choose On the screen: Nudge's pointer travels to each field, button, tab and menu, presses it for show, and explains each step in a box while the rest of the screen is dimmed.

Knows your database

It studies your database every day: the custom modules and Studio fields, how each app is configured, and where the work really is. When a topic is covered by a standard app and by your own module, it answers from the one your people use.

Does the work, as you

In Agent mode it finds records by what you call them, filters and groups your lists, counts and sums, approves, confirms and validates, creates and changes records: with your access rights, and a yes/no card before every change. It never deletes.

Reads your documents

Attach a supplier's PDF and ask for the bill, the RFQ or the purchase order it belongs to. Attach a list of materials and see every likely product, the names need not match, with the stock in each warehouse.

Reports as spreadsheets

Ask for a comparison or a report and choose a spreadsheet: a formatted table with totals and a chart, opened in Odoo's spreadsheet editor, in Documents when you have it.

A senior consultant

It answers as a senior Odoo implementer, accountant, HR, sales or inventory person, cites the official Odoo documentation (included, no internet needed), judges whether your setup fits a contracting, trading, shipping or other business, and says "I don't know" rather than guessing.

Learns from your corrections

Correct it and it writes the lesson down. A manager approves it, and from then on every conversation knows it. Nothing is learnt without that approval.

Speaks your language

Arabic and English, right to left included; Syrian, Egyptian, Gulf Arabic and Arabizi understood. Talk instead of typing, dictate in the fields, have answers read aloud.

Secure by design

  • Every tool runs as the person: their access rights, record rules and companies. What they cannot do in Odoo, Nudge cannot do.
  • Passwords, keys, tokens and secret parameters are never sent to the AI provider.
  • Every change waits for a yes; every action of the agent is in an audit log; budgets per person and per company.
  • Python, SQL and the setup of Odoo for administrators are each behind a switch, off by default.
  • No telemetry: nothing goes to the author, ever.

Your AI provider, your choice

Any OpenAI-compatible provider (Google Gemini, OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral, DeepSeek, a model on your own server with Ollama) or Anthropic Claude. With a local model, nothing leaves your network. Bring your own key: you pay your provider directly.

Keep everything on your own server

For data that must not leave the company, run the model yourself with Ollama: install it on a server of yours, pull an open model (Qwen, Llama, Gemma, Mistral), and add its address under AI Providers, no key. Questions, screens, files and what the agent reads stay on that server. Count on a GPU: a model of 14–32B parameters on a card with 16–24 GB of memory answers well; smaller models are weaker at planning and at using the tools, and a server without a GPU is slow. Odoo.sh cannot host it: the model runs on a separate server Odoo can reach.

Useful before any key

Until a provider is set up, the panel searches the screens you can open, the settings of your apps (for administrators) and the official Odoo documentation, and the pointer shows the way to what it found. No AI is asked.

Works with

  • Nudge: Community and Enterprise.
  • Nudge: Odoo AI: uses the keys, agents, sources and AI tools of Odoo's own AI app (Enterprise).
  • Nudge Spreadsheets: with Odoo's Spreadsheet; Nudge Spreadsheets in Documents: with Documents (Enterprise).

Getting started

  1. Install Nudge.
  2. Nudge ‣ Configuration ‣ AI Providers: paste your key, press Test Connection.
  3. Settings ‣ Users: give people the group Nudge / User (talk and be shown), Agent User (Nudge does the work), or Manager.
  4. Press the avatar at the bottom of any screen, or the shortcut, and ask.

The user guide (English and Arabic), the security and privacy notes and a test plan are in the doc folder of the module.
