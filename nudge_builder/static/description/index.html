Six questions, three designs, then a website of draft pages

Nudge Website Builder asks about the business, shows three directions of the home page side by side, and, once you pick one, builds the pages from Odoo's own snippets. Nothing is published until you say so.

The interview

Your business and languages

What the business is (or a sector template to start from), the website, and the languages of the site, in Arabic and English.

Colours, fonts, pages

The colours of the logo (read from it when there is one), fonts that suit Arabic and Latin, and the pages wanted: about, services, projects, team, contact, blog, FAQ, privacy and terms.

A shop or not, and the tone

An online shop when Website Sale is installed, and the tone of the writing. The text comes from your company's own facts, or from the sample copy of a template when no AI provider may be used.

Three directions, side by side

Before anything is built, Nudge draws three versions of the home page with your colours and fonts, on the sample text of the template; your own words are written when the pages are built. Pick one, or ask for the directions again. Then Build this website makes the pages, the menu, the theme colours and fonts through Odoo's own customisation, and the privacy and terms pages.

The four shop templates add categories, filters, banners, notes for the product page and for checkout. Pictures come from the provider's picture model when you set one, otherwise from placeholders drawn for this module; the names in the templates are placeholders (Your Company, Raghad, Ebrahim) to replace with your own.

The online shop

With Website Sale installed, a shop template shows a card of what Nudge would arrange: product categories, filters, the layout of the shop, banners and the menu. You say yes or no. A short readiness list then tells what is still yours to do, such as payment and delivery.

A check of any website

Site Check reads a website, built by Nudge or not, with no AI: titles and descriptions, broken links, missing translations, heavy images, contrast and alt text, privacy and terms pages, and the shop. Each finding has a severity, a count and Show me. The fixes only fill what is empty or add an unpublished draft, and only after you have seen the list.

Safe by design

  • Nothing is published until you press Publish all pages. No existing page is edited and nothing is deleted.
  • It runs with your own rights, in your companies. It is for a Nudge manager who is also a website designer.
  • The privacy and terms pages are a starting template: have a lawyer read them before the site is published.
  • Private mode applies: the picture model and the writing never go to a provider outside your server when it is on.
  • Needs Nudge and Website installed (Odoo 17, 18 or 19); Website Sale is optional.
