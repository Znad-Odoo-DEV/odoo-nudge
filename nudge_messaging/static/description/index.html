Ask your Odoo from your phone

Nudge Messaging answers your questions on WhatsApp and Telegram, and prepares your changes: you say yes inside Odoo, never in the chat.

Three promises

As the person, never as an administrator

Each person links their own phone with a one-time code from their preferences. What the phone asks runs with that person's rights and companies. A phone nobody linked gets one polite line and nothing else.

Nothing changes from the chat

"Make a quotation for Raghad" comes back as a link. You read what will be written and press Yes in Odoo. Voice notes and pictures of documents are prepared the same way.

Everything is kept

Every message is a request in What Nudge Did, marked WhatsApp or Telegram, with its steps and outcome. The budgets, private mode and the limit of answers at the same time apply.

What you can say

Ask

"كم فاتورة متأخرة؟" "How much did Raghad Trading buy this month?" Short answers, compact lists, and an Open in Odoo link for the record. Any Arabic dialect, and English.

Ask for a change

"اعمل عرض سعر لراغد" "Create a task: call Ebrahim tomorrow". Nudge prepares it and sends the approval link. Send a photo of a bill and it prepares the draft.

Connected in three steps

  1. An administrator turns it on in Settings, Nudge, Messaging assistants, and enters the keys of WhatsApp Business Cloud API and/or a Telegram bot (kept, never shown again).
  2. Meta and Telegram are told the addresses shown there. Telegram has a one-click Register the Webhook.
  3. Each person opens Preferences, Nudge Messaging, gets a code and sends it from their phone.

Safe on your server

  • Every message is proved genuine (signature or secret token) before anything is read; the body is capped at 1 MB; a message is taken once.
  • 20 messages in 10 minutes per sender by default.
  • Private mode: only what needs no AI answers; voice notes and pictures are refused.
  • Installs Nudge: MCP Server too, switched off until an administrator turns it on. Voice notes need ffmpeg on the server and a provider that transcribes.
  • Community and Enterprise. No new Python package, no extra service: plain Odoo HTTP. Odoo 19 for now; 17 and 18 are not ported yet.
