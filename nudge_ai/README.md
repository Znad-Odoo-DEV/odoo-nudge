# Nudge for Odoo AI

A bridge between Nudge and the AI app of Odoo Enterprise. Requires Odoo Enterprise (`ai`).

- The documents, links and articles given to the AI agents as sources are searched by Nudge when it answers,
  for the people who may read them.
- What the agents are told (their instructions and topics) is told to Nudge too, so that the company's own
  guidance applies.
- The server actions marked "Use in AI" are at the agent's hand, each one shown to the person before it runs.
- A Nudge provider can take the key set in the AI app: typed once.

Installs itself where both Nudge and the AI app are installed. Odoo 19.
