# Nudge: Discuss

Write `@Nudge` in the chatter of any record, or in a channel of Discuss, and ask: "@Nudge summarise this",
"@Nudge who has to approve it?". Nudge answers there, in a few seconds, as an internal note the other employees
read too.

- It answers as the person who asked, from what they may read of the record: its fields, the records it links
  to, the last messages. It looks up nothing else, so that an answer read by the whole team holds nothing taken
  from elsewhere; only the official documentation of Odoo may be searched.
- Nothing is changed from the chatter: for that, Nudge points to its panel, in Agent mode, where every change
  waits for a yes.

Depends on `nudge` and `mail`. Installed from Apps by an administrator who wants it; never by itself.
Community and Enterprise. Odoo 17, 18 and 19.
